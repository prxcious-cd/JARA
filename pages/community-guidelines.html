Community Guidelines

JARA ∆ is built on trust. These guidelines keep our campus marketplace safe, honest and useful for everyone.

Be Honest

  • List only real products and services you actually have or offer.
  • Use accurate photos — your own, not images from Google or other sellers.
  • Set honest prices. Do not bait-and-switch after contact.
  • Describe your item accurately — include condition, defects and limitations.
  • Do not impersonate other sellers, businesses or JARA staff.

Respect Everyone

  • Treat buyers and sellers with courtesy and respect.
  • No harassment, threats or abusive messages to any user.
  • No discrimination based on gender, tribe, religion or background.
  • If a deal does not work out, walk away — do not escalate.
  • Respect people's time. If you are not buying, say so.

What Is Not Allowed

  • Counterfeit, stolen or illegal goods of any kind.
  • Weapons, drugs, alcohol or any controlled substances.
  • Explicit, adult or pornographic content.
  • Scams, fake listings or fraudulent payment requests.
  • Spam — duplicate listings, fake engagement or bot activity.
  • Listing items you do not own or have no intention of delivering.
  • Collecting payments without delivering goods or services.

Stay Safe

  • Meet in public, well-lit areas on campus for exchanges.
  • Never share your bank PIN, passwords or OTPs with anyone.
  • Verify payment before handing over any item.
  • For large transactions, bring a friend or meet at a campus office.
  • If something feels wrong, trust your instincts and report it.

PRO Sellers

  • PRO status comes with higher visibility and trust — use it responsibly.
  • Maintain the same honesty and quality standards as all other sellers.
  • Repeated violations will result in PRO status removal without refund.
  • JARA reserves the right to verify any PRO seller's products or services.

Enforcement

  • Listings that violate these guidelines will be removed without notice.
  • Repeat violations will result in account suspension.
  • Serious violations (fraud, harassment) result in permanent bans.
  • JARA admins review all reports and act within 24–48 hours.
  • Appeals can be sent via WhatsApp to the JARA support number.
